# Invoicer

Invoices to artists at each of their four stages, as the studio's own branded page to print or save as a PDF, with a short email to go with it. It has its own place at the left end of the navigation panel, on its own, in no group.

## What's new
- **Every invoice as a PDF file, everywhere (19 Oct 2026).** A real PDF download, before it's sent and after, at any stage:
  - **Invoicer:** **PDF** above the invoice downloads it as it's shown (its stage and look). The new invoice's live preview has **PDF** too, before it's even made.
  - **Portal → Money:** **PDF** on every invoice.
  - **The client:** **Download PDF** on their invoice page, and a "Download the invoice (PDF)" link in every email (the receipt too). Share's message has the PDF link.
  - **Agent:** knows each invoice's PDF link and gives it when asked.
  - If the PDF can't be made just then, the invoice opens ready to print instead (Save as PDF), so the button always gives a PDF.

- **Monzo on every part of an invoice** (19 October 2026), once the studio's Monzo link is set (**Bank details** here, or Portal → Money):
  - the invoice, its page, its PDF, its email (a Pay with Monzo button) and its text version, as before;
  - the copy saved in Google Drive (Pay with Monzo, with the amount filled in);
  - **Share**: the WhatsApp / text message gets the Monzo link too, and **Copy Monzo link** copies it on its own;
  - Portal → Money: **Copy Monzo link** on each sent, unpaid invoice;
  - **Record a payment → Monzo**, so a receipt says it was paid with Monzo;
  - the Agent knows each unpaid invoice's Monzo link, and Nova Bot's chat tells artists they can pay an invoice with Monzo.
  - Each link opens Monzo with the amount due now (the deposit, or what's left) and the invoice number as the reference.
- **Before or after the session** (19 October 2026): each invoice says which, in **+ New invoice** (and Portal → Money, and the Agent), and its emails follow the studio's rules for client emails:
  - **Before: deposit to secure the date.** Welcoming and excited about the session. It says the date is only held once the deposit is paid, gives the deposit and its due date clearly, then what happens next. The rest is due on the session day.
  - **After: full payment.** Warm and thankful: thanks for the session and how well it went, then the full amount and its due date. No deposit is asked for.
  - With a deposit, once the session day comes, the balance email is the same thank-you, with what's left after the deposit.
  - Everything in them is filled in from the invoice (the artist's first name, the session from its lines, the amounts and dates), so there's nothing to fill in. A message with a gap still in square brackets, like "[Client Name]", is never sent.

## How it works

- **Invoices** (left):
  - Every invoice, newest first, with its total and where it is: Deposit due, Balance due, Overdue, Paid receipt, or To approve (not sent yet).
  - Search by the artist's name or the invoice number (NS-0001…).
  - The buttons under the search show one kind at a time.
- **The invoice** (middle):
  - The four stage buttons. The one marked **NOW** is where the invoice really is, worked out from its payments and its due date:
    - nothing paid yet: Deposit due;
    - some paid: Balance due;
    - past the due date with money still owed: Overdue;
    - all paid: Paid receipt.
  - Press another stage to see the invoice as it would be at that stage.
  - The page is the studio's own (the look above). Each stage has its own words and colour:
    - **Deposit due:** violet, "Action required: 50% deposit due to secure your booking". The total, the deposit due now and the balance due later.
    - **Balance due:** charcoal, "Balance payment notice". The total, each payment with its date, and the balance left.
    - **Overdue:** crimson, "Urgent notice: this invoice is past due". An Overdue notice badge, and the due date in red with how many days late it is.
    - **Paid receipt:** green, "Thank you for recording with us!". It becomes a RECEIPT with a large PAID stamp, and "£0.00 (paid in full)".
  - **PDF** downloads it as an A4 PDF file with the colours kept (to print it, **Open** it and use its Print button). **Open** shows it in its own tab.
- **Email** (right):
  - The subject and message for the stage shown. You can change the words.
  - **Copy email** copies both, to paste into Gmail.
  - **Send…** does what's possible for that invoice. Every email waits for an admin's Approve:
    - **Not sent yet:** an admin can press **Approve and email it**: one press (the line under it says who it goes to). It emails the invoice with a link to its page, where the artist can pay.
    - **Sent, not fully paid:** **Send for approval** queues this message as a reminder, with a button to the invoice's page. An admin approves it here or in Portal → Money.
    - **Paid:** receipts aren't emailed from here yet. Copy the email, download the receipt with **PDF** and send them from Gmail.
- **+ New invoice:**
  - The artist (names from the client list fill in their email) and the services from the studio's price list. The sessions come from the booking rules, so a price changed there changes here too.
  - Mixing, mastering and production are on the list too. Mastering is £30 a song for 3 to 10 songs and £50 otherwise; it switches by itself. Custom production is "from £250": type the price agreed.
  - Something else adds a line of your own.
  - **Before or after the session:** before, a deposit secures the date; after, it's the full amount (the deposit line goes and the due date is when payment is due).
  - Also: the issue date, the day the balance is due (the session day), and a note for the invoice.
  - The total and the 50% deposit show as you go.
  - The new invoice waits for an admin's Approve before anything is emailed.

- Every sum is worked out by Nova Bot in whole pence (src/invoicer.js there), never by an AI.
- The deposit is always 50%, the booking rules' DEPOSIT_PERCENT.
- The studio's details on the page come from Portal → Money's settings. Where they're empty, it uses:

  - +44 20 8291 3028;
  - info@novacane.co.uk;
  - www.novacane.co.uk.
- Bank details show only when they're filled in, in Money's settings.
- There's no VAT line unless VAT is set in Money's settings.
